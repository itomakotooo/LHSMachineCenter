KEY DISCOVERY -- pay_id 8 (bar_mixed) engine behavior
================================================================================

Critical mistake in brief math: pay_id 8 (line_3_group, mult 2×) fires when
ALL 3 payline cells are bars (1bar/2bar/3bar) or wild AND not 3-of-same
after wild substitution. NOT just strict {1bar, 2bar, 3bar} triples.

Engine evaluator tests:
  (1bar, 2bar, 3bar)  -> pay_id 8 mult 2.0x
  (1bar, 1bar, 2bar)  -> pay_id 8 mult 2.0x  (not 3-of-same → not pay_id 7)
  (1bar, 2bar, dd)    -> pay_id 8 mult 4.0x  (wild boost; not 3-same)
  (1bar, 2bar, 2bar)  -> pay_id 8 mult 2.0x
  (1bar, 1bar, 1bar)  -> pay_id 7 mult 5.0x  (pure bar1)
  (1bar, dd, 1bar)    -> pay_id 7 mult 10.0x (wild boosts to 3-bar1)

P(pay_id 8) scales roughly as (sum_bars)^3, NOT 6 × p1 × p2 × p3.

This bloats ge1_lt5 RTP by ~10-14pp at any bar configuration that can
support ge5_lt10 ≥ 8pp via bar1 pure pays.
