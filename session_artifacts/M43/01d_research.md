## Wild + respin + mini-game mechanism (as documented in industry sources)

### Wild substitution
- **Base Lucky Ducky (Class II original)**: industry reviews are explicit that the duck icon is the "unique symbol" — but no consistent industry citation describes the duck as substituting for other symbols in the base Class II game. The "Lucky Ducky icon" appears to be a top-prize symbol on the payline, not a wild.
- **"Lucky Ducky 2" sequel**: explicitly described as having "a wild symbol, scatter symbol, and 1x multiplier" plus red-screen respin. Source: https://www.gamblingsites.com/slots/lucky-ducky/ (mentioning Lucky Ducky 2 in the Lucky Ducky review).
- **Caveat for Stage 4**: M43's `configs/machines.json` `logicClassNames` reportedly include wild substitution. The Analyst (Stage 1c) will need to reverse-engineer from rawdata which family of Lucky Ducky M43 implements. The industry literature does not give the substitution rules of either Class II Lucky Ducky version precisely — paytable per-combination payouts are not published anywhere I could find.

### Red Screen respin (matches M43 `WinReSpin*` logic class names)
- **Trigger**: occurs randomly after a winning spin. Approximately 20% of winning spins trigger.
- **Mechanic**: screen turns red; the reels respin automatically 1 to 5 times. Each respin is **guaranteed to be a winning spin** with payout ≥ the triggering win.
- **Award scaling**: larger triggering win → larger expected respin award (positively correlated per https://slotsguy.com/hals-vgt-red-screen/).
- **Retrigger**: Lucky Ducky specifically supports retrigger (https://www.aboutslots.org/vgt/lucky-ducky/ — "there is a retrigger feature that allows you to win even more along the way").

### Pick a Duck (mini-game / second-screen bonus)
- **Trigger**: random after a Red Screen event; "the top case of the cabinet slides open, revealing a carnival setting with ducks swimming around in a pool"; player picks a duck for a credit award.
- **Source**: https://www.gamblingsites.com/slots/lucky-ducky/ ; https://www.aboutslots.org/vgt/lucky-ducky/.
- **Related Bonus Blast pool** (across VGT Lucky Ducky cabinet configs): Pick a Duck / Victory Lap / Winning Wheel / Pick a Safe / Pick a Pot / Rainin' Rubies / Lucky Pluck. Up to 8 distinct configurations. Rainin' Rubies bonus is documented at 200 to 200,000 credit range across 12 award outcomes.

### Class II bingo backend (important caveat)
- The original VGT Lucky Ducky is **Class II under the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act**. Outcomes are determined by a backend bingo draw, not by a per-spin RNG on weighted reels. The reels are visual representations of bingo-pattern matches.
- **Implication for M43 archetype framing**: the player-perceived archetype (3-reel 1-line classical Vegas slot with cherry/bar/seven plus respin + pick mini-game) is faithful to Lucky Ducky. The underlying math model (weighted reels with explicit symbol probabilities like RWB) is **not** the original Lucky Ducky's actual implementation. For M43 the team should target the **player-visible experience** Lucky Ducky delivers and not try to replicate Class II bingo internals (which would require a network of bingo balls + pattern matches).
- Sources: https://wizardofvegas.com/forum/gambling/betting-systems/29661-class-ii-slot-machines/ ; https://www.goodgamblingsites.com/articles/how-do-class-ii-slot-machines-work/ ; https://blog.dimebit.com/how-vgt-slot-machines-operate-and-pay-out/.

**Designer at Stage 3.5 needs to consider**: with mode 1 target = 95% RTP (4 pp higher than RWB's 87% baseline AND higher than Lucky Ducky's typical 91-93%), the "high-multiplier bucket more share" user direction may need to come from:
- Boosting top-jackpot frequency (e.g. red-7+blue-7+seven 1-in-N tighter than RWB's 1-in-262,144), OR

- Restructuring family share toward the seven family

The 95% target is **above** the documented Lucky Ducky baseline. This is a meaningful design tension to surface at Stage 3.5 contract drafting.

## Gaps / caveats

1. **No public PAR sheet for Lucky Ducky** — neither slotgamedesign.com, easy.vegas, nor Wizard of Odds publishes one. Class II bingo machines rarely leak PAR data because the underlying math is bingo-draw-based, not weighted-reel-based, and the reel symbols are only a display layer. This is the largest gap. **Designer must rely on Stage 1c rawdata reverse-engineering** for the actual paytable + symbol weights M43 implements.

2. **Hit rate for Lucky Ducky specifically is not published** — sources call it "high win frequency" qualitatively. Stage 3.5 should anchor hit rate using either (a) classic 3-reel 1-line industry benchmark 10-30% or (b) RWB's 17.35% as a cousin proxy or (c) M43's own rawdata hit rate from Stage 1b §1, with the caveat that current "稀烂" state may already reflect the wrong number.

3. **Wild substitution rules unclear in literature** — original Lucky Ducky (Class II) does not appear to have a wild based on industry reviews; Lucky Ducky 2 has a wild + scatter + 1x multiplier but specific substitution and payout rules are not published. **M43's logicClassNames suggest wild substitution is implemented** — this is one place where M43 may have diverged from the original Lucky Ducky design or may match Lucky Ducky 2. Stage 1c Analyst can determine which by checking rawdata, and Stage 4 Designer should note explicitly which variant family M43 belongs to.

4. **Respin mechanic — Class II vs Class III nuance** — original Lucky Ducky's Red Screen respin is "random after a winning spin" with bingo-pattern dependency. A weighted-reel implementation (M43) would need a different trigger model: either (a) probability conditional on win amount or (b) a separate scatter-trigger model. Stage 1c Analyst should determine M43's actual trigger model from rawdata; the industry "~20% of winning spins" figure is a starting reference, not a precise specification.

5. **Mini-game (Pick a Duck) — payout distribution unclear** — only Rainin' Rubies (a sister VGT Bonus Blast game) has documented payout structure (200 to 200,000 credits across 12 outcomes). Pick a Duck specifically has no published payout distribution. Stage 1c should determine M43's mini-game payout distribution from rawdata.

6. **One precise RTP source (91.06%) is single-cite** — gamblingsites.com is the only place I found a 91.06% precise figure for Lucky Ducky. All other sources give a range. Confidence on this exact number is low; the 91-93% range is well-supported, but the specific 91.06% should be treated as one data point not a confirmed number.

7. **User's archetype hint mislabeled vendor (IGT)** — to be flagged at Stage 3.5 contract sign-off as a friendly correction. This doesn't change the design (Lucky Ducky is well-known regardless of which name the user used), but does change which industry baseline to cite (VGT 91-93%, not IGT 87-94%).

8. **No academic-grade study on Lucky Ducky specifically** — Harrigan/Dixon's PAR sheets paper (https://stoppredatorygambling.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/PAR-Sheets-Probabilities-and-Slot-Machine-Play-Implications-for-Problem-and-Non-Problem-Gambling.pdf) covers IGT class-III games; Lucky Ducky / VGT is not in its dataset. The Lucas-Singh CV-vs-time-on-device 2008 paper applies to any volatility class but doesn't give Lucky Ducky-specific numbers.

## Verdict

- **Confidence in archetype identification**: **High**. Lucky Ducky = VGT, 3-reel, 1-line, cherry/BAR/double-BAR/triple-BAR/red-7/blue-7/Lucky-Ducky-logo classical symbol set, Red Screen respin (~20% of winning spins, 1-5 free spins, guaranteed floor), Pick a Duck mini-game (random after Red Screen), 10,000x top jackpot. Class II bingo backend in original (but M43 uses weighted-reel engine — the relevant archetype is the player-visible experience, not the bingo math).

- **Confidence in industry RTP / hit-rate numerical anchor**: **Medium**. RTP range 91-93% is well-attested (multiple sources). Exact 91.06% is single-source. Hit rate not published for Lucky Ducky directly; 17.35% RWB cousin + 10-30% classic 3-reel range serve as proxies.

- **Confidence in paytable / per-symbol RTP share**: **Low**. No PAR sheet for Lucky Ducky exists publicly. RWB family-share data (sevens ~48% / bars ~31% / cherry+other ~21% of total RTP) is the closest cousin anchor. M43 must rely on Stage 1c rawdata reverse-engineering for actual paytable + symbol weights.

### Key actionable findings for Stage 3.5 contract drafting

1. **Mode 1 RTP target (95% ± 1) sits 2-4 pp above Lucky Ducky's industry baseline** (91-93%). This is a deliberate design choice and the Boundary Contract should explicitly note it — Designer should cite either "deliberately higher RTP than archetype for player-time-on-device retention" (Lucas-Singh 2008 CV-vs-time cross-cite) or escalate to user. Designer should not silently target 95% as if it were the archetype baseline; the baseline is ~92%.

2. **High-multiplier bucket share request** maps to two architectural choices Designer must surface to user at Stage 3.5 Step 7:
   - **Option A**: boost 7-family share (mirror RWB ~48% of RTP in sevens, with M43 top-prize 1000x+ contributing the high-bucket share) — classical boom-bust profile;
   - **Option B**: keep base spin distribution moderate and route the high-multiplier share through the Red Screen respin layer (which has a guaranteed floor mechanic and ~20% winning-spin trigger rate baked in) — this is more faithful to Lucky Ducky's actual archetype experience.
   - Industry baseline supports either, but Option B is more "Lucky Ducky-authentic". Designer Stage 3.5 should pose this tradeoff to user.

3. **Wild substitution + respin trigger model** is under-documented industry-wide and **must come from Stage 1c rawdata reverse-engineering**. Designer should NOT propose contract §2 boundary values for wild contribution or respin trigger conditional probability until Stage 1c is in; if Stage 3.5 starts before Stage 1c finishes, those bounds should be marked TBD-pending-1c rather than guessed from industry numbers.
